    Body Drive
    
    
    Your hands are the steering wheel, your feet are the pedals. Your camera watches you with MediaPipe Pose — no controller needed.
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Set up
    
        
      
          
    1. Prop your phone or webcam 2–3 m away, at about hip height, so your hands, hips and right foot are in view.
      2. 
          
    2. Stand (or sit back) in a driving stance, right foot flat on the floor, arms out as if gripping a wheel at 9 and 3.
      3. 
          
    3. Press Start with camera, then C to calibrate. Redo it whenever you move.
      4. 
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Controls
    
        
    
          
    SteerTilt your hands like turning a wheel, or slide them left / right
    
          
    One handShow just one hand out as if gripping the wheel: move it outward / inward to steer. The other hand is ignored
    
          
    ThrottleLift your right foot (knee up)
    
          
    BrakeLift the right foot and sweep it toward your centre
    
          
    Auto-pilotTap the Auto-pilot button on the player phone (or P). It drives flat out and brakes for bends. Brake to take over
    
          
    GearsAutomatic: the car changes gear for you
    
          
    Keyboard←→ steer · ↑ throttle · ↓ brake
    
          
    Other keysV view · P auto-pilot · C calibrate · R reset · H debug

    This screen shows the game. You get a 5-letter code (and a link) to enter on the Player phone. Your body movements are sent here in real time; the keyboard also works as a backup.
    
      
    
        
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    The camera feed is processed entirely in your browser and never uploaded. In Player/Viewer mode only body-pose numbers (no video) travel between your two devices, directly where possible; a free public PeerJS server is used just to introduce them. First start downloads the pose model (~9 MB).
