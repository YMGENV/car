    Body Drive
    
    
    Your hands are the steering wheel, your feet are the pedals. Your camera watches you with MediaPipe Pose — no controller needed.
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Set up
    
        
      
          
    1. Prop your phone or webcam 2–3 m away, at about hip height, so your whole body — hands to feet — is in view.
      2. 
          
    2. Stand (or sit back) in a driving stance, both feet flat, arms out as if gripping a wheel at 9 and 3.
      3. 
          
    3. Press Start with camera, then C to calibrate. Redo it whenever you move.
      4. 
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Controls
    
        
    
          
    SteerTilt your hands like turning a wheel, or slide them left / right
    
          
    ThrottleLift your right foot (knee up)
    
          
    BrakeLift the right foot and sweep it toward your centre
    
          
    ClutchLift your left foot — then the gearbox shifts for you
    
          
    Keyboard←→ steer · ↑ throttle · ↓ brake · Shift clutch
    
          
    Other keysV view · T gearbox mode · Q/E shift (manual) · C calibrate · R reset · H debug · Y stalling
    
        
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
      
      
    
    
    
    The camera feed is processed entirely in your browser and never uploaded. First start downloads the pose model (~6 MB).
